# Video and sound playbook: what the whole video and its audio show

Built 2026-11-17 from the product's own corpus. It follows `LAUNCH_PLAYBOOK.md` (a launch post with a video beats
the same account's launch posts without media in 65% of comparisons) and `VIDEO_PLAYBOOK.md` (first frames only).
This time the videos themselves were analysed: length, what is on screen across the video, pace, captions, and sound.

## Read this first

**No trait of the video or its sound could be confirmed on launch videos alone.** There are 779 launch videos from
409 accounts; that gives 150 to 400 same-account comparisons a trait, enough to see an effect of 12 points or more
and nothing smaller. Nothing reached that bar.

What follows is one level weaker (grade C): patterns that hold across **all 4,975 video posts of 556 company and
founder accounts**, launch or not. Use them as defaults for video in general. For launch videos they are plausible,
not shown.

How to read the numbers:

- Every comparison is between two video posts of the same account. Outcome: likes against the account's usual
  (median of its previous 30 posts).
- *Win rate*: of two videos by one account, one with the trait and one without, how often the one with it did better.
  50% is no effect. *Likes ratio*: likes with the trait over likes without. *Adjusted*: the same with the post's
  wording, layout, link, post type, video shape and (for sound) the kind of picture held fixed. Brackets are 95%
  ranges from resampling accounts.
- A pattern is listed only if all three numbers exclude "no effect". The rule was written down before any number
  was computed. 53 traits were tested, so one or two of the weaker patterns could be chance.
- Likes only. Associations inside accounts, not experiments. None could be confirmed on its own after 23 April 2026
  (662 posts).

## 1. Length: aim for one to two minutes; avoid clips under 15 seconds (C)

| Length | Share of videos | Win rate against other lengths | Likes ratio | Adjusted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 15 s | 22% | 43.7% (40.4 to 47.2) | 0.76x (0.67 to 0.86) | 0.82x (0.73 to 0.92) |
| 15 to 30 s | 21% | 47.9% (44.9 to 50.8) | 0.95x | 0.97x |
| 30 to 60 s | 23% | 52.8% (50.0 to 55.7) | 1.05x | 1.04x |
| 60 to 120 s | 19% | 56.4% (52.8 to 59.8) | 1.31x (1.17 to 1.48) | 1.25x (1.13 to 1.40) |
| Over 120 s | 15% | 48.4% (44.2 to 53.0) | 1.00x | 0.96x |

5,045 comparisons from 291 accounts for the 60 to 120 second row. Launch videos point the same way without
passing: 61.7% (46.4 to 75.4), 1.37x (1.10 to 1.74), 288 comparisons. Clearer in accounts under 100,000 followers
(1.50x, 1.19 to 1.92) than above (1.13x, 0.90 to 1.37).

## 2. What is on screen

- **A person talking to the camera for most of the video does worse than other kinds, in large accounts (C).**
  All accounts: 45.2% (41.9 to 48.6), 0.81x, 4,960 comparisons, 275 accounts. Against a produced film (motion
  graphics, titles, footage): the film wins 57.3% (53.0 to 61.7), 1.40x (1.20 to 1.63). At 100,000+ followers the
  person-led video wins 37.1%; under 100,000 it is level (52.7%, range includes 50%). Small accounts lose nothing by
  putting the founder on camera.
- **A screen recording of the product is average**: 51.1% (47.9 to 54.2), 0.98x. It is 41% of all videos. Showing
  the product at some point, or in the first 3 seconds, makes no measurable difference in either direction.
- A face on screen for half the video or more: 45.6% (41.7 to 49.5), 0.83x. A face at any point: no effect (51.9%).

## 3. Pace: cut the video; one continuous shot does worse (C)

- At least one change of scene against none: 55.6% (52.3 to 58.8), 1.26x (1.12 to 1.41), adjusted 1.20x; 5,518
  comparisons, 306 accounts. 23% of videos have none.
- More than 20 changes a minute (one every 3 seconds) against fewer: 55.3% (52.0 to 58.7), 1.20x, adjusted 1.23x.
- The measure is coarse: the files hold 2 frames a second, so it tells "static", "some changes" and "constant
  change" apart, not exact cuts, and it under-counts changes inside white-background screen recordings.

## 4. Sound

Videos autoplay muted, so each sound trait was also tested between videos of the same visual kind.

- **Sound against silence: not established.** 53.1% (49.9 to 56.5), 1.15x; 1.02x (0.90 to 1.14) once length is
  held fixed, because silent videos are mostly short clips. A video with a voice does no better than a silent one
  (49.4%).
- **Among videos with sound, music without a voice beats a voice (C).** No speech against speech: 58.0% (54.2 to
  61.8), 1.36x (1.18 to 1.58), adjusted 1.23x (1.08 to 1.41); 57.1% (51.5 to 62.7) between videos of the same visual
  kind; 3,142 comparisons, 225 accounts. Music alone against a voice alone: 62.1% (56.6 to 68.1), 1.66x, adjusted
  1.48x (1.20 to 1.80); same in small and large accounts. Launch videos: 77.6% (58.2 to 91.2) on only 40 comparisons.
  The likely reading is not that people listen: a music bed marks an edited product film, a bare voice marks a talk
  or a walkthrough.
- A wide loudness range (8 LU or more; a mix with quiet and loud passages): 54.9% (50.7 to 58.9), 1.19x, adjusted
  1.18x. Weak, and the same caveat applies.

## 5. No gain found: do not spend effort here

| Trait | Result across all video posts |
|---|---|
| Burned-in captions | 44.7% (40.9 to 48.7), 0.87x. The detector is right 72% of the time and captions go with talking-head videos, so this says "no gain found", not "captions hurt" |
| Product UI in the first 3 seconds | 49.9% (46.4 to 53.2). Among announcements, videos without it did better (59.9%, 51.3 to 68.4; 1.31x) but one test fails |
| A title card or logo in the first 3 seconds | 54.9% (51.7 to 58.1), adjusted 1.07x (0.97 to 1.18) |
| A change of scene inside the first 3 seconds | 53.3% in general, 45.1% (38.0 to 50.9) among announcements: no direction |
| Loudness (loud, quiet) | 52.4% and 48.2% |
| Music tempo (120 beats a minute or more), busy music | 52.6% (49.4 to 55.7); 49.0% |
| Speech rate; first word within 2 seconds | 46.7%; 43.6% (38.2 to 49.4), ratio range includes 1 |
| Music under a voice, against a voice alone | 54.0% (49.1 to 59.0), adjusted 1.14x (0.95 to 1.37) |
| Spoken opening line: names the product, states a problem, a number, an announcement verb | 53.8%, 46.4%, 53.5%, 55.9%; all ranges include no effect once the picture is held fixed |
| Slideshow of stills | 36 videos: cannot be measured |

## 7. The model

Giving the forecasting model the video's length, kind, pace, sound and a summary of its frames did not improve its
picks in a way that holds up: +1.3 points on 848 held-out pairs of videos (range -0.3 to +2.9), no change on 6,397
video-against-other-post pairs, and worse on launch pairs with a video (-2.4, range -6.6 to +0.5). The product's
forecasts are unchanged.

## What is still open

Launch videos need about three times as many posts to confirm or reject any of this at the launch level. Sound was
never checked by ear. Views are not available for video posts.
