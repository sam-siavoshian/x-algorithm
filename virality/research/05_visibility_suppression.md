# 05 — Visibility suppression ("anti-virality") in the X For You repo

Repo: `/home/user/x-algorithm`. All paths below are relative to that root unless absolute.

Orientation notes that affect everything else:

* There is **no `visibility-filtering/rules/registry.rs`** in this checkout (the README links to it, but the file is absent). The evaluation-order "registry" is `visibility-filtering/rules/levels.rs`; the rules themselves are `rules/tweet_rules.rs` and `rules/author_rules.rs`; action precedence is `rules/action_kind.rs`.
* Fold order: for each safety level the **user list is folded first, then the post list**; the first *withholding* action (Appealable / Drop / Tombstone) ends evaluation; interstitials and engagement limits fill "slots" and do not stop the fold (`visibility-filtering/rules/catalog.rs:133-137`, `rules/action_kind.rs:59-131,154-158,170-175`). Severities: Appealable 18 > Drop 17 > Tombstone 16 > LabelInterstitial 11 > MediaRestriction (blur) 10 > SoftIntervention 8 > LimitedEngagement 6.
* Audiences (`rules/rule_spec/rule.rs:100-115`, `rules/rule_spec/engine.rs:75-76`): `Everyone`; `NonAuthors` = `!ViewerIsAuthor`; `NonFollowers` = `!ViewerIsAuthor && !ViewerFollowsAuthor`; `OnlyAuthor`.
* Home-mixer calls VF with level **`TimelineHome` for in-network candidates and `TimelineHomeRecommendations` for out-of-network candidates, ancestors and quoted posts** (`home-mixer/candidate_hydrators/vf_candidate_hydrator.rs:70-105`). `TIMELINE_HOME_RECOMMENDATIONS` is a strict superset of `TIMELINE_HOME` (`levels.rs:493-498`), so the extra OON rules are exactly the "drop only when recommended to a non-follower" set.
* `VFFilter` removes candidates whose action is `Drop`, `Tombstone`, or `NotEvaluated` (a VF failure = drop); `Allow`, `Interstitial`, `Avoid`, `Downrank` are kept **with no score change** (`home-mixer/filters/vf_filter.rs:22-27`). No rule in these levels emits `Downrank`/`Avoid`, so VF never applies a rank penalty on For You — it is binary (drop / keep) plus interstitials.
* `AncillaryVFFilter` removes a post whose ancestor, quoted or reposted post was itself dropped (`home-mixer/filters/ancillary_vf_filter.rs:69-72`; computed at `vf_candidate_hydrator.rs:118`). `DedupConversationFilter` keeps only the highest-scored candidate per conversation (conversation id = smallest ancestor id, else the original tweet id) (`home-mixer/filters/dedup_conversation_filter.rs:94-124`).

## 2. Author behaviours that raise suppression probability

| Behaviour | Exact logic |
|---|---|
| **Posting frequency / bursts** | BDSM `TweetSpamBot` head (flywheel labels `TWEET_CREATE_BURST`, `QUOTE_TWEET_SPAMMER`, `CONTENT_AMPLIFIER`, `bdsm/runtime/heads.py:259-268`). Features: burst count = other actions within 1 s in a ±5 window (`bdsm/rust/abuse-v3-features/src/derived.rs:26-39`), timing regularity `1/(1+std(Δt))` (`:111-117`), entropy of action types, repetition ratio (`:138-146`). Curiously, hard enforcement **skips users whose actions are ≥50% `SERVER_TWEET_CREATE`** (`score_results_sink_focal.py:829-845`); those go to the spam-bounce challenge lane when the dominant client is official (`:1100-1162`). Grox PTOS also runs on min-traction/min-impression streams (`grox/flows/ptos/constants.py:1-13`). |
| **Link posting** | URL reputation verdicts drive the hardest labels: `BAD` → `SPAM` (drop everywhere, incl. retroactively on every tweet with that URL); `UNSAFE` → `MALICIOUS_URL`+`DO_NOT_AMPLIFY`+`SEARCH_BLACKLIST`; `LOW_QUALITY` anywhere in redirect chain → `SPAM_HIGH_RECALL` (unless last hop GOOD/WHITELIST) (`LQ_..._v2.bot:8-9`). **Pinning** a post with a BAD/LOW_QUALITY link gives the *account* `SPAM_HIGH_RECALL` for 7 days, re-checked on every follow event (`PinnedLowQualityOrBadUrl.bot`, `FollowFromActorWithPinnedLowQualityOrBadUrl.bot`). Card images judged near-perfect NSFW taint the URL for 7 days for every poster. |
| **Duplicate text** | BigQuery clusters on unigrams / CJK characters → `COPYPASTA_SPAM` on all cluster members, excluding promo cards and high-PageRank authors (`BBQDuplicateTextProd.bot:16-36`). Also `DUPLICATE_CONTENT` (notifications). |
| **Reply spam** | Grox reply-ranking LLM (prompts withheld): score 0 → `RISKY_HIGH_VIZ_REPLY`; only replies to non-self roots are scanned, coordinated-spam scan only when root author > 180k followers (`grox/flows/reply_spam/task_filter.py:14-96,98-159`); scarecrow 25026 spamScore ≥ 0.97; BDSM `ReplySpamBot` (hard suspend when > τ and dominant reply client is an official app, `score_results_sink_focal.py:920-932`); enforcement `fast_reply_spam_post`, `bb1_reply_spam_bot_*`. In For You, OON replies/reposts are dropped outright unless `EnablePhoenixOonReplies` (default false) (`home-mixer/filters/oon_retweet_reply_filter.rs:609-622`; `param.rs:68-78`); in-network replies only shown if to the root or a followed user (`thunder/posts/post_store.rs:340-367`). |
| **Follow churn** | BDSM `FollowBot` flywheel labels `FOLLOW_UNFOLLOW_CYCLE`, `PURE_FOLLOW_API_BURST`, `FOLLOW_FARM_BOT` (`heads.py:211-223`); `EngagementAmplifier` includes `FOLLOW_THEN_FAV/REPLY_PIPELINE`. FollowBot enforcement is skipped if unfollows > follows, for new accounts and starter-pack follows (`score_results_sink_focal.py:837-843,847-903`). Default action for `enforcement_threshold_reached` is **suspension** (`enforcement_user.yaml:184-189`). user-cred-v2 also drops edges between investigated "linked" accounts (`UserCredV2App.scala:102-118`). |
| **Engagement bait** | PTOS `SpamEngagementBaiting` / `SpamEngagementFarming` / `SpamCardManipulation` → `SPAM_HIGH_RECALL` **with no high-PageRank exemption** (`...result_sink.py:233-248`) → OON drop. |
| **New account** | Author < 72 h (`decode/author.rs:159`) only matters for notifications filters. Viewer-side: new viewers (<24 h) lose `SOFT_NSFW` OON posts; `NewUserMinEngagementFilter` can require a fav/engagement/view floor on OON posts for viewers < 30 min old (default off, threshold 0, `param.rs:852-904`). BDSM gates FollowBot by account age. Abuse-enforcement has no age exemption. |
| **Low user-cred score** | No score ⇒ no *protection*: every scarecrow rule, grox label writer, safety-label-user-agg rule and the abuse-enforcement precheck carve out high-PageRank authors (see §4). Non-high-PageRank authors also get the full 7-day NSFW account labels instead of 3-day/report-only (`ApplyNsfwUserLabelOrCreateReport.df:52-72`). |
| **High report / block ratio** | agatha `RateBasedLabels`: for each account, `score = (I + 0.1) / (I + F + 0.1/prior)` where `I` = distinct users (≤100 sampled per reporter) who blocked / reported (AbuseTriageTool, 180 d) / spam-reported (tattle types DM, ReportAsSpam, UserReport, TweetReport; 30 d) the account and `F` = distinct **out-of-network** favoriters (30 d); `prior` = global ratio with per-user sums capped at 1000 (`RateBasedLabels.scala:459-487,489-548,550-614`, `LabelUtil.scala:188-220`, `Favs.scala:122-156`). On the ranking side, predicted report/block/mute/not-interested carry weights −234 / −31.2 / −58.8 / −53.07 (`home-mixer/params/param.rs:509-527`), applied to *predicted probabilities* (`xai-value-model/scoring.rs:29-55`). |

## 3. Content properties that trigger labels

* **NSFW media**: media-model-proxy adult model → `precisionNewNsfw`/`recallNewNsfw` thresholds 0.95 / 0.999 / 0.8568428 (non-video) / 0.6 (video) (`botmaker-rules/scarecrow/derived-feature/IsHighPrecisionNsfwNewModelLogic.df`, `IsNearPerfectNsfwNewModelLogic.df`, `IsHighRecallNsfwNewModelLogic.df`). Grox PTOS (LLM over text + media; prompts withheld) adds `NSFW_HIGH_RECALL`/`NSFW_HIGH_PRECISION`/`SOFT_NSFW`. `pnsfwmedia/` combines CLIP media embeddings with agatha account scores (README). Three NSFW posts in a day ⇒ account label.
* **Gore**: violence-and-gore model (media-model-proxy `decider.yml:34`) via bot 20616 thresholds above; PTOS `ViolentMedia`.
* **Hateful imagery**: hateful-symbol model exists (`media-model-proxy/config/decider.yml:30`) and `TakedownReason::HatefulImagery` exists (`visibility-filtering/models/tweet.rs:120`) but no published rule maps it to a label; it is ignored by the takedown country match.
* **Known-bad media hashes**: `PDNA` label (PhotoDNA) → drop everywhere (`tweet_rules.rs:28-33`); fingerprinting models v1/v2 in media-model-proxy (`decider.yml:14-18`).
* **Spam text patterns**: duplicate-text BigQuery clusters (`COPYPASTA_SPAM`); enforcement `gibberish_post`, `llm_slop_post`/`llm_slop_user` (LLM-slop detector, 30d/7d), `SpamEmbeddingPtosDistilled`, `SpamEmbeddingMajorityPoster` (`enforcement_post.yaml`, `enforcement_user.yaml`); grox PTOS spam categories. `HIGH_SPAMMY_TWEET_CONTENT_SCORE` exists as a label type but no rule reads it.
* **Specific keywords**: only viewer-side muted keywords (`ViewerMutedKeywordFilter`); `NSFA_KEYWORDS_HIGH_PRECISION` is an ads-brand-safety label (`home-mixer/models/brand_safety.rs`), not organic reach. Grox keyword prompts are withheld.
* **URL domains**: URL-reputation store verdicts (`GOOD`/`WHITELIST`/`LOW_QUALITY`/`BAD`/`UNSAFE`/`NSFW_CARD_IMAGE`) evaluated over the full redirect chain (`scarecrow/legacy/UrlVerdictRegistry.scala:13-80`); card URLs are expanded too (`GetExpandedUrlsForTweet.df`). Verdict changes are applied retroactively to every tweet that used the URL.
* **Language mismatch**: not a suppression signal anywhere; the only language condition is `FOSNR_ABUSE_INSULTS` requiring `post.language == "en"` (`...result_sink.py:260`).
* **Media + subscriber-only**: `NSFW_HIGH_PRECISION` on exclusive (subscriber) content is dropped at render for everyone but the author (`tweet_rules.rs:433-442`).

## 4. user-cred-v2: computation and consumption

Computation (`user-cred-v2/UserCredV2App.scala`):
1. Nodes = users from the usersource snapshot excluding restricted/deactivated/suspended/erased (`ValidUserInfo.scala:660-666`); `isNearZero` = `user_state == 1` (`:658,670`).
2. Edges = Flock follows with state 0 (`Edge.scala:623-631`), minus any pair flagged by account-expansion investigations as linked accounts (`UserCredV2App.scala:102-118`). Only non-near-zero users may be edge sources (`:287-318`).
3. Teleport vector = `(1-β)·uniform over premium (blue/gold/grey/org/affiliate) non-near-zero users + β·engagement`, β = 0.5, where engagement mass to author `a` = Σ over engagers `e` of `prior_mass(e) × (favs+retweets e→a)/(total favs+retweets by e)` over the last 7 days (`:141-193`, `UserCredV2Config.scala:559,569`).
4. Power iteration with jump probability α = 0.2, warm-started from last snapshot, until Σ|Δmass| < 0.001 or 50 iterations: `new = normal·(1-α) + p·α + p·missing·(1-α)` (`:320-390`, config `:564-566`).
5. `score = clamp(165.2 + 7.07·ln(mass), 0, 100)` (`UserCredV2.scala:692-699`); snapshot guarded by row-count/mass/"VIT ≥ 55" drift gates (`SnapshotSafeguard.scala`); published to HDFS + Manhattan (`UserCredV2App.scala:453-485`).

Consumption — **protection, not ranking**:
* `IsHighPageRankUser` = `score >= 54` or `PREDICTED_HIGH_PAGE_RANK` label; fallback when no score: followers > 25 000 (`IsHighPageRankUser.df:3-18`, `HighPageRankThreshold.df`). Every published scarecrow rule that labels content/accounts carries `!IsHighPageRankUser(...)` (plus `AutoExpiringTempSkipList` and grey-verified exemptions).
* Grox: PTOS skips high-PageRank/grey-badge authors entirely (`grox/flows/ptos/task_filter.py:73-86`) except engagement-bait spam types; reply spam exempt at `userCredScore >= 66` (`grox/flows/reply_spam/constants.py:9`, `task_write.py:39-45`).
* abuse-enforcement-service: `cred.is_high || cred.score >= <threshold>` and `cred.follower_count >= <threshold>` are leading **skip** rules for both user and post actions; thresholds published as mock `12.34` "to reduce gaming" (`enforcement_user.yaml:19-33`, `enforcement_post.yaml:21-35`; `strato.rs:134-140`).
* safety-label-user-agg: `highPageRankOrGreyBadge` exclusion (`...Processor.strato:490-516`).
* Not consumed by home-mixer ranking, VF rules, Phoenix ranking features, or OON eligibility (grep over `home-mixer`, `visibility-filtering`, `phoenix` finds only an unused `user_credibility` thrift field in the multimodal embedding server, `phoenix/crates/serving/xai-recsys-mm-server/src/events.rs:660`).

## 5. Distribution outside For You ("ghost ban" surfaces)

* **Search**: `SEARCH_BLACKLIST` (UNSAFE links) and `AGATHA_SPAM` (agatha spam scores; file name says Top-tweet) are written by scarecrow but read by nothing in this repo — they act on search. `Thunder` schema carries `SpamSignalType::SEARCH` (`thunder/schema/tweet.rs:342-348`).
* **Replies / conversations**: `RISKY_HIGH_VIZ_REPLY` (grox score 0, scarecrow 25026, LLM-slop, BDSM `HideReply`) and the grox reply-ranking score written to Manhattan (`task_write.py:143-148`) feed conversation ranking; `DOWNRANK_SPAM_REPLY` + `DownrankSpamReply` account label hide replies in notifications for UQF viewers.
* **Notifications**: `NOTIFICATIONS_WRITER_V2[_MENTION|_REPLY]` drop on `AgathaSpamTopUser`, `EngagementSpammer`, `BlinkWorst`, `NotGraduated`, `DownrankSpamReply`, `SpamHighRecall`, `Compromised`, `ReadOnly`, `ImpersonationHighPrecision`, `AbusiveHighRecall`, plus viewer quality filters (new account < 72 h, default avatar, no email/phone, not followed-by) (`levels.rs:74-100,165-264`).
* **Immersive video**: `IMMERSIVE_EXPANDED_RECOMMENDATIONS` additionally drops any NSFW-labelled post/author for viewers without the sensitive-media setting (`levels.rs:461-486`).
* **In-network timelines**: Thunder caps posts per followed author (`max_per_user`, `post_store.rs:292,367`) and filters replies to non-followed users; `SelfReplyChainFilter` in the reverse-chron pipeline drops self-reply chains directed at unfollowed users (`home-mixer/filters/self_reply_chain_filter.rs:346-362`, used in `reverse_chron_posts_pipeline.rs:158`).
* **Account-level enforcement**: abuse-enforcement-service actions = suspend, `SpamHighRecall`, `Arkose`/`Captcha`/`SpamLivenessCheck` challenges (`decision.rs:280-296`); appeal holds via abuse-ledger-service.
* **Transparency**: Under the Hood reports the post labels in `underTheHoodLabels.strato:3-111` and account labels `:119-184` + takedowns; it does **not** surface `RISKY_HIGH_VIZ_REPLY`, `COPYPASTA_SPAM`, `AGATHA_SPAM`, `SEARCH_BLACKLIST`, `SOFT_NSFW`, `POSSIBLY_NSFW_ACCOUNT`, `AgathaSpamTopUser`, `EngagementSpammer`, `BlinkWorst`, `NotGraduated`, `DownrankSpamReply`.

## 6. Ranked "do not do this" list for a marketer who wants reach

1. **Do not post links to domains/redirects with BAD or UNSAFE reputation (or shorteners that resolve to them).** `SPAM` = dropped for *everyone* and applied retroactively to every post with that URL (`Tweet_Spam_High_Recall_RTF_All_Bad_URL_Sources.bot:3-47`, `tweet_rtf_or_unrtf_on_bad_verdict.bot`); UNSAFE = `MALICIOUS_URL`+`DO_NOT_AMPLIFY` (OON drop) + search blacklist.
2. **Do not pin a post containing a LOW_QUALITY/BAD link.** 7-day account `SPAM_HIGH_RECALL`, re-triggered on every new follower (`PinnedLowQualityOrBadUrl.bot`, `FollowFromActorWithPinnedLowQualityOrBadUrl.bot`) → all posts hidden from non-followers (`levels.rs:66`).
3. **Do not post engagement bait / engagement farming / card manipulation.** PTOS labels these `SPAM_HIGH_RECALL` even for high-PageRank accounts (`...result_sink.py:233-248`) → OON drop.
4. **Do not post adult or gory media, even once "borderline".** Any `NSFW_HIGH_PRECISION`/`HIGH_RECALL`/`GORE` post is dropped OON and blurred in-network; 3 NSFW posts in a day, or 3 of the last 5, convert the *account* to `NsfwHighPrecision` for 7 days (`ApplyNsfwUserLabel.df:19-46`, `postToUserLabelRules.strato:395-426`) → every post hidden from non-followers, and SimClusters stops recommending you (`oon_nsfw_simclusters_filter.rs`).
5. **Do not link to pages whose preview card image is sexual.** The URL itself gets `NSFW_CARD_IMAGE` for 7 days and every post carrying it is blurred/dropped OON (`NSFW_Card_Image_URL_to_Tweet_Verdict.bot:16-27`).
6. **Do not copy-paste the same text across many posts/accounts.** Duplicate-text clusters → `COPYPASTA_SPAM` on all members (`BBQDuplicateTextProd.bot`), plus `DUPLICATE_CONTENT`/`SpamEmbeddingMajorityPoster` → `SpamHighRecall` 30d (`enforcement_user.yaml:131-136`).
7. **Do not mass-reply, especially to big accounts or via API/automation.** Grox reply-spam scoring, `RISKY_HIGH_VIZ_REPLY` (14–30 d), BDSM `ReplySpamBot` hard-suspend when the dominant reply client is official and score > τ (`score_results_sink_focal.py:920-932`); OON replies never reach For You anyway (`oon_retweet_reply_filter.rs`).
8. **Do not run follow/unfollow, like-drip, or like+repost "pipelines".** BDSM heads `FollowBot`/`LikeBot`/`EngagementAmplifier`/`RTBot`; the default outcome of a fired threshold is suspension (`enforcement_user.yaml:184-189`), with challenges on the cusp band (`:156-175`).
9. **Do not accumulate blocks/reports faster than out-of-network likes.** agatha `(I+0.1)/(I+F+0.1/prior)` (`RateBasedLabels.scala:474-486`); crossing 0.98–0.999 percentile scores loses notifications (`AGATHA_SPAM_TOP_USER`) and search (`AGATHA_SPAM`); the ranker also prices predicted reports at −234 vs likes (`param.rs:527`).
10. **Do not post gibberish or LLM-slop filler.** `gibberish_post` → post `SpamHighRecall` 30d; `llm_slop_user` → account `SpamHighRecall` 7d (`enforcement_post.yaml:45-50`, `enforcement_user.yaml:118-123`).
11. **Do not target insults at people (English).** `FOSNR_ABUSE_INSULTS` → OON drop + "limited visibility" notice to followers (`tweet_rules.rs:169-199`); the four reviewed FOSNR labels restrict the post to your profile.
12. **Do not mark your account or posts "sensitive" unless you must.** `nsfw_user` flag ⇒ OON drop of everything, blur in-network, no SimClusters, dropped for logged-out/underage viewers (`author_rules.rs:155-160`; `conditions.rs:15-23`).
13. **Do not rely on reposts/quote-storms for OON reach.** OON reposts are filtered pre-ranking (`oon_retweet_reply_filter.rs:620`); `QUOTE_TWEET_SPAMMER` is a `TweetSpamBot` flywheel label; `RetweetDeduplicationFilter`/`DedupConversationFilter` collapse repeats.
14. **Do not operate networks of linked accounts to inflate credibility.** user-cred-v2 removes edges between investigated linked pairs and only premium accounts seed the teleport mass (`UserCredV2App.scala:110-118,173-175`); without a high score (≥54) you have no exemption from any label rule.
15. **Do not edit posts repeatedly or promote-only (nullcast) and expect organic reach.** Superseded edits and nullcast posts are dropped at every level (`tweet_rules.rs:215-232`).

Positive corollary visible in code: genuine credibility (user-cred ≥ 54 / ≥ 66, grey badge, PREDICTED_HIGH_PAGE_RANK) is the single biggest *protective* factor — it exempts an account from almost every automated label, downgrades NSFW account labels to 3 days or to human review, and short-circuits abuse-enforcement to `skip`.
